Movement
Hopscotch at home — hopping
Chalk or tape squares. Hop from square to square on one foot. Count how many hops in a row without putting the other foot down.
Language
Tell me what happened
After any event: 'what happened at the beginning? What happened next? At the end?' Expand whatever they say.
Thinking & Play
Sort with a rule switch
'First animals vs vehicles — now let's sort by colour.' Can they switch the rule? If not yet, just celebrate the first sort.
Feelings & Friends
Notice and name a feeling
Cooperative challenge at home: 'let's move all the cushions to the bedroom together.' Count how many trips. Celebrate the completion: 'we did it — together!'
Independence
Teach the jacket floor flip
At a non-urgent time — not the morning rush. Once they have it, they have it forever.
This Week's Book
Possum Magic
By Mem Fox. Four-to-five word sentences throughout — matching the sentence expansion theme. Australian content and setting. Model the sentence structure as you read; pause at descriptive moments and wait for your child to add a word.
