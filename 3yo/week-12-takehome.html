Movement
Draw lines and a face together
Ask: 'can you draw a face?' A circle with two dots and a line is a genuine milestone. Praise the specific achievement.
Language
Make a word book
Photographs of 10 things your child can name. Look at it together each day. Add to it every week — the program ends, the language growth continues.
Thinking & Play
Run one routine without prompts
Just the song or signal. Notice how much they can do independently now. That's 12 weeks of practice.
Feelings & Friends
Notice and name a feeling
Talk about the Play Pods friends at home — look at the group photo, name them. Ask about them. Community doesn't end when the program does.
Independence
Keep one regular helper role
The program ends. The routine continues. They are capable. They know it.
This Week's Book
Where the Wild Things Are
By Maurice Sendak. Emotionally rich vocabulary, narrative complexity, imagination — a celebration of 12 weeks of language and emotional development. Read it together and ask: "Have you ever felt like Max? Where in your body did that feel like?"
