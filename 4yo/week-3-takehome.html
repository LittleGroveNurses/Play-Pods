This week, focus on:
Facilitator: tick 1–2 areas for this family to prioritise at home this week.
Movement
Practise catching from further away
Start close with a soft ball, then take a step back each time they catch it.
Language
Play a turn-taking game
Any game with a clear turn order — even rolling a dice back and forth.
Thinking & Play
Try three-step instructions
"Get your bag, put your shoes on, then come sit down." Break it into two plus a pause if it's too much.
Feelings & Friends
Clap out the syllables in names
Start with their own name — cla-p once per syllable together.
Independence
Practise buttons on a big shirt
An old adult shirt with big buttons is much easier to learn on than small ones.
