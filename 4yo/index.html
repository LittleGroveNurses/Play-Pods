4-Year-Olds

Play Pods

A 12-week parent-coaching group program for 4-year-olds with developmental vulnerability. Five evidence-based pods per session across motor, communication, cognitive, social-emotional, and self-help domains, with a focus on pre-literacy, complex play, and school-readiness.

Session Weeks

Skipping (Intro) + Balance + Locomotor Warm-Up
Complex Sentences with "Because"
2
Throwing for Accuracy
Asking and Answering 'Why'
3
Catching Two-Handed, Greater Distance
Following Three-Step Directions
4
Stairs — Alternating Feet
Story Retell — Beginning, Middle, End
5
Pedalling a Tricycle or Scooter
Category Vocabulary
6

Using Past and Future Tense
7

Galloping and Skipping Refinement

10
Ball Skills — Dribbling and Catching a Smaller Ball
Answering 'How' and Predicting Outcomes
11

5+ Word Sentences with Connectors
12
Consolidation — A Full Skills Showcase
Vocabulary and Sentence Celebration

Evidence Frameworks

🏃
Motor
FMS — Fundamental Movement Skills, plus a graded balance and locomotor-variation thread across the term. Evidence & branding note →
🗣️
Communication
OWL — Observe, Wait, Listen (responsive interaction), plus rotating spatial-concept and vocabulary threads. Evidence & branding note →
🧠
Cognitive
Pre-literacy (letters, print conventions) and numeracy threads run across the whole term, alongside weekly reasoning and memory skills.
❤️
Social-Emotional
CASEL 5 + emotion coaching (Gottman, 1997), tracking the shift from Autonomy to Initiative across the term. Evidence & branding note →
🙌
Self-Help
OT ADL Framework, building toward school-readiness independence in dressing, hygiene, and daily routines.
