This week, focus on:
Facilitator: tick 1–2 areas for this family to prioritise at home this week.
Movement
Draw a person together — no example first
Let them draw their own way before showing them how — it's their own body knowledge, not a copying task.
Language
Notice a feeling in someone else
"Look, they seem a bit sad — I wonder why?" Practising empathy in everyday moments.
Thinking & Play
Give a three-step instruction during play
"Get the red block, put it on the blue one, then knock it down."
Feelings & Friends
Sort objects by two features at once
Big AND red, not just one or the other.
Independence
Practise mealtime manners together
Sitting, waiting, asking, and saying thank you — a few consistent habits matter more than a long list.
